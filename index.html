🔍 Recommendation Trust Auditor

A lightweight prototype for reviewing whether online recommendations are supported by specific, balanced, and credible evidence, or whether they may be shaped by visibility, repetition, promotional language, or weak source context.

1. Research Context

2. Paste Comments or Review Excerpts

Method Note

This prototype does not scrape live platform data, verify individual claims, or use external APIs. It is designed as a policy-oriented research workflow: users paste review excerpts, and the tool structures the evidence, identifies reliability concerns, and labels uncertainty before a recommendation is trusted.
